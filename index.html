Frequently Asked Questions

Does it work entirely offline?

Yes. The Industrial Edition is built with an "Offline-First Architecture". Once you activate the license via our portal or offline key file, it is securely cached and works without any internet connection, making it perfect for secure factory floors.

Do I need KUKA WorkVisual installed?

No! KUKA KRL Professional operates completely independently in VS Code. It uses its own custom AST parser and static analyzer, meaning you can develop on any OS (Windows, macOS, Linux) without WorkVisual.
